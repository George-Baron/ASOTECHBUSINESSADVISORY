# ASOTECH Business Advisory — Brand Guidelines

**Tagline:** Control. Accuracy. Intelligence.
**Positioning:** Comprehensive business solutions for a smarter, more efficient business: consulting and advisory, software design and development, inventory management, business systems, operational excellence.

## Palette

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| ASOTECH Navy | `#0B2F4F` | Primary: headings, navigation, sidebars, footers |
| ASOTECH Orange | `#F28C1E` | Calls to action, highlights, the logo swoosh |
| Cloud | `#F3F6FA` | Page backgrounds |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, content surfaces |
| Slate | `#5B6B7B` | Secondary text, borders, table headers |
| Success | `#1E9E5A` | In-stock, approved |
| Warning | `#C2640A` | Low stock, pending (AA contrast on white) |
| Danger | `#C0392B` | Errors, variances, rejected |

Palette follows the supplied ASOTECH banner (navy and orange). It replaces the teal and amber palette in the original draft prompt.

Contrast: navy on white is about 13:1 and white on navy about 13:1. White text on orange is only about 2.4:1, so orange buttons use navy text (`#0B2F4F` on `#F28C1E` is about 5.4:1).

## Logo

- `logo.svg`: horizontal lockup (icon, ASOTECH wordmark, BUSINESS ADVISORY line).
- `icon.svg`: the "A" with ascending bars and orange swoosh, for app headers, social avatars, sidebars.
- `favicon.svg`: compact tile version for browser tabs.

Clear space: keep at least the height of one ascending bar around the logo. Minimum width 120 px (icon only: 24 px). Do not recolour, stretch, rotate or add effects. On navy backgrounds use the white-wordmark variant (swap `#0B2F4F` text for `#FFFFFF`).

## Typography

Inter (fallback: Segoe UI, Arial). Headings 700–800, body 400–500. Wordmark tracking +1 px; sub-line tracking is wide (~0.3 em).

## Voice

Professional, direct, evidence-led. No invented client names, results or testimonials. Demo data is always labelled "Demo".
