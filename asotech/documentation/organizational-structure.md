# Organisational structure

Board/Founders -> Managing Director/CEO -> three divisions:
- **Consulting:** business advisory, inventory consulting, accounting advisory, audit and reconciliation, HR consulting, startup consulting.
- **Technology:** software development, product design and UI/UX, website and app development, POS and inventory systems, integrations, QA and infrastructure.
- **Corporate services:** finance and administration, HR and admin, legal and compliance, business development, client services, marketing and sales.

Departments A to J and their responsibilities are listed in `project-blueprint.md` section 3. Role names are a plan, not a statement of current staff: do not publish names or titles of real people until confirmed.
